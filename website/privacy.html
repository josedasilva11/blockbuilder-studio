Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 May 2026

TL;DR, The app makes zero network requests. The website has zero analytics, zero cookies, zero third-party trackers. We collect your email if you buy a licence (for receipt + key delivery) or subscribe to the newsletter (for release notes). That's it.

What we do not collect

  • No telemetry in the desktop app. It does not phone home, not on launch, not on any action, not on close.
  • No analytics on this website. No Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no Plausible, no anything.
  • No cookies set by this website, beyond what your browser sets locally for things like dark-mode preference (these never leave your machine).
  • No fingerprinting, no IP logging beyond standard CDN-level logs that Cloudflare retains for 24h.

What we do collect

If you buy a commercial licence

Lemon Squeezy is the payment processor. They collect your name, email, billing address, and payment method to process the transaction and the EU VAT MOSS filing. We receive your name and email from Lemon Squeezy via webhook, use them to:

  • Sign your unique licence key (your email is included in the signed payload, this is how the app verifies the key offline).
  • Email the key to you via Resend (transactional email provider).
  • Honour any future support request you make about the purchase.

We do not add you to any marketing list, do not share your information with anyone, do not analyse your usage. The key validation happens entirely on your machine, Marjers cannot tell when or how often you use the app.

If you subscribe to the newsletter

Email goes to Buttondown. You'll receive release notes when there's a new BlockBuilder Studio version. One-click unsubscribe. We don't email you anything else.

If you email us

The email you send and the address you send it from are retained as long as the conversation is active, plus a reasonable archive period (12 months).

Where data lives

  • Lemon Squeezy stores your purchase record on their servers (US + EU).
  • Resend stores the transactional email log (EU).
  • Buttondown stores the newsletter subscriber list (US).
  • Cloudflare serves this website and proxies licence webhooks (global CDN).
  • Your project files (.json, .stl, .obj, .step) live exclusively on your machine. The app never uploads them.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you're in the EU/EEA, you have the right to access, correct, export, and delete the personal data we hold about you. Write to geral@marjers.com and we'll handle it within 30 days. We can also issue a fresh licence key if you've lost the email containing yours.

Contact

Marjers, sole-trader business operating from Portugal. Questions about this policy: geral@marjers.com.
